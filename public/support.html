Questions, problems or suggestions? Email support@figure.tvr.works.

How to calculate

In Figure, open … → Help. Search for a task or browse a topic for short steps and worked examples. Your calculation stays in place while you read.

Report a calculation problem

Include:

  • The mode, numbers, units and exact keys you pressed.
  • The answer you expected and the answer Figure showed.

Report a display or app problem

Include your iPhone model, iOS version and Figure version. For layout problems, note whether the phone was upright or sideways and whether you use larger text. A screenshot helps.

Leave out client details, addresses and other private information you do not want to share. See our privacy policy for how support messages are handled.
