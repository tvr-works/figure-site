Construction calculator for iPhone.

Work in feet, inches and fractions or metric. Solve triangles, circles, offsets, roofs and stairs with diagrams beside your values.

Convert units, reuse Recent Values, and find practical steps in Help without losing your calculation. Calculations and Help work offline, with no account or ads.

Figure is in testing. An App Store link will be added when it is available. Requires iOS 26 or later.
